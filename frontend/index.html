Methodology

How the Checker Works

A deterministic, AI-assisted pipeline designed to evaluate policies against statutory requirements.

1

Text & Table Extraction

Uploaded PDFs or DOCX files are parsed server-side. All narrative clauses and table cells are cleanly extracted.

2

Structured 23-Item Checklist

The backend evaluates evidence against predefined requirements derived from Act No. 9 of 2022 & Act No. 22 of 2025.

3

Gemini AI Analysis

The AI compares document clauses against each requirement and labels them Met, Partially Met, or Not Addressed.

4

Deterministic Scoring

Overall percentage is calculated deterministically: Met = 1.0 pt, Partially Met = 0.5 pt, Not Addressed = 0.0 pt.

5

Remediation Priorities

High-severity gaps are highlighted first with concise action items.

⚠️ Prototype & Demonstration Project

Project Scope & Legal Notice

HeyPDPA! is an experimental toy project and educational prototype. It was created to demonstrate automated gap detection concepts and is not an official compliance audit tool. No output constitutes binding legal advice or formal certification.

🏷️ Arbitrary Severity Weightings

Severity labels (High, Medium, Low) and scoring point values are demonstration heuristics assigned without formal regulatory backing or gazetted consensus.

⚖️ Not Legal Advice

This software does not provide legal opinions or regulatory clearance. Compliance determinations require qualified legal counsel and data protection specialists.

📜 Commencement Schedules

Provisions of the PDPA follow phased commencement dates via government Gazette notices. Some provisions are not yet legally in force.

🤖 AI Verification

AI text models may overlook subtle nuances or misinterpret complex clauses. Results must always be verified manually against the primary legislation.

How It Works

Our requirements analysis strategy, explained.

1 Document Upload

You upload a governance or security document (PDF or DOCX). The system extracts all readable text, including content inside tables.

2 Checklist Comparison

The extracted text is compared against a structured checklist of PDPA (Personal Data Protection Act, Sri Lanka) requirements, covering lawful basis, data subject rights, security obligations, breach notification, DPO appointment, and more.

3 AI-Assisted Analysis

An AI model reviews the document against each requirement and determines whether it is Met, Partially Met, or Not Addressed, with a short explanation for each.

4 Compliance Score

An overall compliance percentage is calculated: fully met requirements count fully, partially met requirements count half, and unaddressed requirements count as zero.

5 Recommendations

The system highlights the most important, actionable next steps — prioritizing high-severity gaps first.

Note: Many provisions of Sri Lanka's PDPA are not yet legally in force. This tool assesses documents against the Act's requirements regardless of current enforcement status, to help organizations prepare ahead of time. See the Requirements tab for the full list, including current enforcement status for each.

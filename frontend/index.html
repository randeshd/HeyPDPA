Try it See how it works

How it works

1

Read

The text and tables are pulled out of your PDF or DOCX on the server.

2

Stamp

Google's Gemini checks the text against each of the 23 requirements and gives it a stamp.

3

Add up

Points are totalled, divided by 23 and shown as a percentage, with suggestions for the biggest gaps.

MET

Clear evidence in the document. 1 point.

PARTIALLY MET

Some evidence, but incomplete or unclear. Half a point.

NOT ADDRESSED

No evidence found. No points.

(Total points ÷ 23) × 100 = compliance %

Every requirement counts the same. Severity (High, Medium or Low) doesn't change the score. It only suggests which gaps to look at first.

The 23 requirements

Everything the checker looks for, drawn from Act No. 9 of 2022 and Act No. 22 of 2025. Open one to see the evidence it expects.

HeyPDPA! is a toy project and an educational prototype. Nothing it produces is legal advice or a formal certification.

Made-up weightings

Severity labels and point values are demonstration heuristics, with no regulatory backing.

Not legal advice

Real compliance decisions need qualified legal counsel and data protection specialists.

Phased commencement

PDPA provisions commence in phases through Gazette notices, and some are not yet in force.

AI can be wrong

AI models can miss nuance or misread clauses. Check results against the Act itself.
